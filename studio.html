On My Own Terms

Self-Portraits and Essays on Identity, Embodiment, and Everyday Life

Through personal essays and intimate self-portraits, Cassie Soma explores identity, embodiment, love, survival, and the ordinary rituals that make a life feel like home. There are dogs, unmade beds, coffee, and wine. Humor alongside loneliness, tenderness alongside stubbornness, and a woman learning to be seen while holding onto the right to define herself.

A collection in words and photographs: a whole, complicated life, still becoming.

Includes artistic nude self-portraits. Intended for adult readers.

Notes from the Grooming Table

Reflections from Cassie Soma’s life as a mobile dog groomer, bringing together the dogs, the people who love them, and the moments that stay with her after the day’s work is done. Funny encounters, difficult days, and small acts of trust reveal the patience, attention, and care at the heart of the craft.

A personal look at the relationships and everyday experiences that shape a working life, one dog at a time.
