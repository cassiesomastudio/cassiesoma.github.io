What every groom includes

A full-service appointment is tailored to your dog's coat and comfort. A long-haired dog may receive a clipper-based Comfort Groom. A short-haired dog may need no haircut at all.

  • Bath
  • Blow dry and brush-out
  • Haircut when appropriate
  • Ear cleaning
  • Nail trimming
  • Anal glands if needed

Routine brushing is part of every groom. Extra de-shedding is +$10 for a small dog or +$15 for a medium dog when the coat needs more work.

The base price includes a clipper-based Comfort Groom. Shears used to shape the face or head do not add a charge. An extra-long body haircut done with shears adds $15 for a small dog or $25 for a medium dog.

Significant matting is not brushed out. A coat requiring a careful shave-down adds $25.
